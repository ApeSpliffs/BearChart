Membership

The price guide is free and stays free. Membership adds the things that run behind the scenes: your collection on every device, alerts that find bears for you, and 10% off a bear every month at Jay's Retro Exchange.

Questions

Will the price guide stay free?
Yes. Every value, checklist and calculator on BearChart is free for everyone. Membership pays for the work our servers do for you: syncing, alerts, reports and scanning eBay.
How does the Jay's Retro Exchange discount work?
Once each calendar month, take 10% off one Build-A-Bear you buy from Jay's Retro Exchange. Order with the email on your membership and the discount is applied.
Does being a member change what a bear is worth?
No. Members and non-members see exactly the same values, worked out from the same sold prices.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes, in one click from your account page. No emails, no phone calls.
