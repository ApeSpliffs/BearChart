Questions

Can I cancel any time?
Yes, in one click from your account page. You keep membership until the end of the month you paid for.
What does “On the way” mean?
We are building it now. It is not part of what you pay for until it ships, and we will not charge more for it when it does. Anything with a ✓ works today.
Will the price guide stay free?
Yes. Every value and calculator on BearChart is free for everyone, and checklists, the collection tracker and the deal finder are free with an account. Membership pays for the work our servers do for you: syncing and reports today, and alerts and eBay scanning once they are built.
Does being a member change what a bear is worth?
No. Members and non-members see exactly the same values, worked out from the same sold prices.
