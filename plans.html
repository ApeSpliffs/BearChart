Why the free tier is this generous

BearChart is a static site. The whole catalogue and every sold comp arrive in your browser as one file the moment the page loads. Anything worked out from that file — a valuation, a checklist, a deal calculation — is happening on your machine with data you already have, so putting it behind a paywall would be a lock on a door with no wall attached. Anyone could step around it from a developer console in a few seconds.

So the split is not basic versus good. It is what your browser can do on its own against what needs a server: keeping your collection somewhere other than one browser, holding a schedule you can produce again years later, sending a notification, spending API quota to find a listing. Those cost real money to run, they can genuinely be enforced, and that is exactly the set worth charging for.
