Membership

The price guide is free and stays free. Membership adds the things that run behind the scenes: your collection on every device, alerts that find bears for you, and a coupon every month for the Jay's Retro Exchange store.

Questions

Will the price guide stay free?
Yes. Every value and calculator on BearChart is free for everyone, and checklists, the collection tracker and the deal finder are free with an account. Membership pays for the work our servers do for you: syncing, alerts, reports and scanning eBay.
How does the Jay's Retro Exchange coupon work?
Each billing month you get one coupon code for the Jay's Retro Exchange store: $5 off an order over $40 for Pro members, or $10 off an order over $75 for Power members. The order total is counted before tax and shipping. Coupons work on items Jay's Retro Exchange sells itself, not on items listed by other sellers on the Jay's Retro Exchange marketplace. Each code is for one order, cannot be shared or combined with other offers, and expires at the end of that billing month if you do not use it. The full rules are in the terms.
Does being a member change what a bear is worth?
No. Members and non-members see exactly the same values, worked out from the same sold prices.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes, in one click from your account page. No emails, no phone calls.
