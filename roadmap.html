Where BearChart is going

What is built, what is planned, and — just as important — what the available market data will not permit however much work is thrown at it. The full technical blueprint with schemas and algorithms is a separate document; this page is the summary in plain terms.

The constraint everything else follows from

Continuous sold-price data cannot be bought or fetched. eBay's old completed-listings API is retired; sold data now sits behind an application-only programme that is closed to non-partners, with a 90-day window even if granted. Sold listings are behind a login wall, so scraping is both a terms violation and technically blocked. Mercari has no public API.

This is not a gap waiting to be coded around. It is the shape of the problem, and every plan below is designed against it rather than through it. The practical consequence: the sold data BearChart holds will always be data somebody entered — from a Terapeak session, from an order export, or from a sale you made yourself. That is slower than a scraper and it is also the only part a competitor cannot copy.
