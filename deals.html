Deal finder

Current eBay listings priced below what the sold data says the item is worth, after your fees and shipping come out. A gross discount is not profit — the margin column is what you would actually keep.

No sold data, no deal. An item nobody has a comp for cannot be underpriced, because nobody knows what it is worth. Listings are also priced as the condition they are actually in — a cheap ask on a played-with copy is not a bargain because mint ones fetch more.

Asks are judged against a conservative floor, not the median. The thinner the sold data, the further that floor sits below the middle, so an item with three comps has to be a much better bargain than one with thirty before it appears here. That is what stops rare items — the ones with the least data — from filling this page with false alarms.
